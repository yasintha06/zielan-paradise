How it works

1 · EnquiryTell us which tours or dates your clients are interested in, by email, phone or the form at zeilanparadise.com/agents.
2 · QuoteWe send you a written quote for the journey, built around your clients’ dates and group.
3 · ConfirmationWhen your clients are happy, we confirm the arrangements in Sri Lanka.

What a quote contains

  • A day-by-day itinerary
  • Where your clients stay each night, with hotels as listed or similar
  • What is included and what is not
  • The price for your clients’ dates and group size

Every round tour includes

  • Private air-conditioned vehicle (car, van or coach, depending on group size) with an English-speaking guide for the whole trip
  • All hotels as listed (or similar), with breakfast daily; standard check-in is 12:00
  • Meals and experiences named in the itinerary
  • Jeep safaris and boat trips named in the itinerary
  • Airport meet-and-greet, all transfers, and 1 litre of bottled water per person per day
  • A UK-based contact before you travel

Not included: International flights, visa, travel insurance, entrance fees, unless stated, tips.

How our journeys are planned

First night near the airport after a long flight · one direction around the island, no backtracking · two or more nights at most stops · early starts for Sigiriya and safaris · ethical wildlife only: no elephant rides · routes timed to the season, south and west coast December to April, east coast May to September.
