About us
### A love of the island, shared with care
Zeilan Paradise brings together planning from the UK and private chauffeur-guides in Sri Lanka, so your journey feels personal from the very first conversation.
Our story
#### Why we do this
Sri Lanka is small enough to cross in a day, yet it holds ancient cities, cloud forests, leopards, whales and a coastline of quiet coves.
Most visitors only scratch the surface. We started Zeilan Paradise to change that: to show the island through the eyes of people who love it, at a pace that leaves room for the unexpected. Every journey begins with a conversation, and every detail is checked by someone who has been there.
We are deliberately small. That means you deal with the same people from your first enquiry to your journey home, and we can say yes to the requests larger companies can’t.
Vision
##### Sri Lanka, as it deserves to be seen
To be the most trusted way for discerning travellers to discover Sri Lanka: personally, thoughtfully and in comfort.
Mission
##### Journeys built around people
To listen first, then design private journeys that match each guest’s pace, interests and style, and to look after them at every step.
Values
##### Honest, local, responsible
Clear prices, no hidden extras and no pressure. We work with local guides, family-run businesses and ethical wildlife experiences.
Our strengths

#### The itinerary
Day
1
Welcome to Sri Lanka
Negombo
Your chauffeur-guide meets you at the airport and takes you to your hotel in Negombo, 20 minutes away. Rest after the flight and enjoy a sunset dinner by the sea.
Day
2
Into the Cultural Triangle
Sigiriya
Drive north through villages and paddy fields to Sigiriya, home to Sri Lanka's most famous ancient sites. Settle into your jungle hotel and spend the afternoon by the pool.
Day
3
Sigiriya Rock and village life
Sigiriya
Climb Sigiriya rock fortress at dawn, passing the frescoes and lion's paws on the way to the royal summit. Later, cycle through a nearby village, take a short ride on a traditional bullock cart or catamaran, and enjoy a home-cooked lunch with a local family.
Day
4
Polonnaruwa and elephant safari
Sigiriya
Explore the medieval royal city of Polonnaruwa by bicycle, with its palaces, temples and the great carved Buddhas of Gal Vihara. In the afternoon, head out on a jeep safari in Minneriya or Kaudulla to watch wild elephants gather by the water.
Day
5
Dambulla and Kandy
Kandy
Visit the Dambulla cave temple with its ancient statues and painted ceilings, then stop at a spice garden on the way to Kandy. In the evening, attend the ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.
Day
6
Kandy at leisure
Kandy
Stroll through the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya, famous for their orchids and avenues of giant palms. Walk around Kandy Lake and the town in the afternoon, and watch a traditional Kandyan dance performance in the evening.
Day
7
Into tea country
Nuwara Eliya
Climb into the hills past waterfalls and tea estates to Nuwara Eliya. Stop at a working tea factory to see how Ceylon tea is made, with a tasting overlooking the plantations.
Day
8
Horton Plains
Nuwara Eliya
Set off before dawn for Horton Plains National Park and walk the trail to World's End, where the land drops away nearly a kilometre to the plains below. Return to Nuwara Eliya for a relaxed afternoon by Gregory Lake.
Day
9
Through the hills to Ella
Ella
A beautiful drive winds down through tea estates and mountain villages to Ella, with stops at viewpoints along the way. Spend the afternoon exploring the village's cafés and enjoying the valley views.
Day
10
Ella at leisure
Ella
Walk up Little Adam's Peak in the cool of the morning for sweeping views of the hills. Later, visit the Nine Arches Bridge and Ravana Falls, or simply relax.
Day
11
Down to the coast
Mirissa
Leave the hills and drive south to Mirissa, a laid-back beach town lined with palms. Spend the afternoon on the sand.
Day
12
Whale watching
Mirissa
Join an early-morning boat trip with a responsible operator in search of blue whales and dolphins (optional). Spend the rest of the day on the beach or at Coconut Tree Hill.
Day
13
Galle Fort
Mirissa
Take a short trip along the coast to Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk the ramparts, see the lighthouse and browse the boutiques, then return to Mirissa for a final sunset.
Day
14
Departure
After breakfast, your chauffeur-guide drives you to the airport for your flight home.
Optional on request
Swap the day 9 drive for the scenic hill-country train to Ella, subject to seat availability.

## FAQ (`/faq`)

Home
FAQ
Questions
### Good to know
Answers to the questions UK travellers ask us most, from booking and payments to visas and the best time to go.
#### Planning & booking
How does planning a tailor-made journey work?
Tell us when you’d like to travel, who’s coming and what you love, using our journey planner, WhatsApp or email. We reply with questions and ideas, then send a private, day-by-day proposal with hand-picked hotels. We refine it with you until it feels exactly right, and only then do you book.
Can I change one of your itineraries?
Yes. Every journey is private, so our itineraries are starting points. Add beach days, swap a hotel, slow the pace or combine two tours. The proposal is built around you.
How much does a Sri Lanka holiday cost?
It depends on the length of your trip, the season and the style of hotels. Because every journey is tailor-made we price each one individually. Your proposal and quote are free, with no obligation.
What deposit do you need, and when is the balance due?
A non-refundable deposit of 20% of the total cost confirms your booking (some luxury hotels ask for more). The balance is due 60 days before departure. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.
Do you book international flights?
We arrange everything in Sri Lanka: hotels, your private vehicle and chauffeur-guide, transfers and experiences. You book your own international flights, and we’re happy to suggest the best routes and arrival times.
#### Travelling in Sri Lanka
When is the best time to visit?
Sri Lanka has two monsoons, so somewhere is always good. From December to April the west and south coasts, the hill country and the Cultural Triangle are at their best. From May to September the east coast is calm and sunny. We plan your route around the season you travel.
Who will be travelling with us?
Your own private, English-speaking chauffeur-guide drives you throughout, shares the history and wildlife along the way, and is there to help every day. There are no group coaches or shared tours.
Do I need a visa?
Most visitors need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or visa before arrival, and the rules can change. Please check the official Sri Lanka immigration website (eta.gov.lk) and the GOV.UK travel advice for Sri Lanka before you travel. Your passport should be valid for at least six months.
Is travel insurance required?
Yes. Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical expenses, repatriation and cancellation is a condition of booking, and we ask for your policy details before departure.
Is Sri Lanka good for families?
Very. Distances are short, the food is wonderful and children love the safaris, the trains and the beaches. We plan family journeys with gentler days, family rooms and plenty of time to swim.
What about health and vaccinations?
Speak to your GP or a travel clinic about six to eight weeks before you travel, and see the NHS fitfortravel website for Sri Lanka. Drink bottled or filtered water.
What money should I take?
The currency is the Sri Lankan rupee. Cards are accepted in hotels and larger restaurants, and ATMs are easy to find in towns. Carry some cash for small purchases, entrance fees and tips.
