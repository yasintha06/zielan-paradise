## Planning rules

Every package keeps driving to about **4 hours a day or less**, with only a few marked exceptions.

- **First night near the airport** (Negombo) after a long flight, unless the first drive is under 2 hours.
- **No backtracking**: each route runs in one direction around the island.
- **2+ nights at most stops**; one-night stops only as breaks on a long transfer, marked in each plan.
- **No hill-country or Yala/Dambulla-area driving after 6 pm** (mist, narrow roads, wild elephants).
- **Early starts** for Sigiriya and safaris, to beat the heat and crowds.
- **Ethical wildlife only**: Minneriya, Kaudulla, Udawalawe and Yala safaris; no Pinnawala, no elephant rides.
- **Seasons**: south and west coast Dec–Apr; east coast May–Sep; Cultural Triangle and hills work most of the year.
- **Yala Block 1 usually closes for about 6 weeks from September**; check dates before selling Yala tours for that period.
- Drive times in the plans are **estimated door-to-door times by car**, without long stops. Check them on Google Maps, and with a guide once you have one.

**From the ATFL course (Lesson 2):**

- **Plan meal times:** after a morning at Sigiriya, have lunch at a spice garden on the way to Kandy rather than waiting until Kandy.
- **Busy days:** the guide tells clients the departure time for each stop the evening before.
- **Night arrivals:** many UK flights land late at night or early morning; book early check-in in Negombo when needed.
- **Shopping stops only if the client asks.** Never push clients into gem or souvenir shops.
- **Every package needs a unique feature** (an experience, place or activity). It is what makes UK agents look at a new supplier.

**Vehicle by group size** (all private and air-conditioned):

| Group size | Vehicle | Crew |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 people | Car | Chauffeur-guide |
| 4–7 people | Van | Chauffeur-guide |
| 8–20 people | Mini coach | Driver and helper, plus a national guide |
| 21+ people | Coach (33 to 50 seats) | Driver and helper, plus a national guide |

A chauffeur-guide can take up to 7 passengers; larger groups need a separate driver and a licensed national guide. Coaches are slower on hill roads, so allow extra time on hill-country days. Group bands are a guide; confirm with the transport supplier.

## 1. Sri Lanka Highlights — 8 days / 7 nights

The essentials of Sri Lanka in a week, at a relaxed pace with two nights at every stop: the Sigiriya rock fortress and wild elephants, sacred Kandy and its tea hills, and colonial Galle on the south coast.

**Best for:** first-time visitors short on time · **Best time:** December to April

**Zeilan signature experience:** a home-cooked lunch with a farming family near Sigiriya.

**Accommodation summary** (vehicle at disposal throughout; hotels to be chosen)

| Place | Nights | Hotel | Room type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Negombo | 1 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Sigiriya | 2 | To choose (e.g. Heritance Kandalama) | Double / twin |
| Kandy | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Galle | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |

### Day 1 – Welcome to Sri Lanka

Your chauffeur-guide meets you at Bandaranaike International Airport and takes you to your hotel in Negombo, just 20 minutes away. Rest after your flight, with a sunset walk along the beach.

*Drive: 20 minutes · Overnight: Negombo*

### Day 2 – Into the Cultural Triangle

Drive north through coconut groves and paddy fields to Sigiriya. On the way, stop at a village to share a home-cooked lunch with a farming family and see how rice is grown. Relax at your hotel surrounded by jungle.

*Drive: about 4 hours · Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 3 – Sigiriya Rock and wild elephants

Rise early to climb Sigiriya, the 5th-century rock fortress, before the heat and crowds arrive. Pass the painted frescoes and the giant lion's paws on the way to the summit. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park to watch herds of wild elephants.

*Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 4 – Dambulla and Kandy

Visit the Dambulla cave temple, with its ancient Buddha statues and painted ceilings, then stop at a spice garden on the way to Kandy. In the evening, attend the ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 5 – Gardens and tea hills

Stroll through the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya, then visit a tea estate in the hills above Kandy to see how Ceylon tea is made, with a tasting. In the evening, watch a traditional Kandyan dance performance.

*Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 6 – Down to the south coast

Travel south to the coast, stopping for a riverside lunch at Bentota, then continue to Galle. In the late afternoon, walk the ramparts of Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and watch the sunset from the walls.

*Drive: about 4 hours · Overnight: Galle*

### Day 7 – Galle and the beach

Explore the fort's lanes, lighthouse, old Dutch church and boutique cafés, then spend the afternoon on a nearby beach such as Unawatuna.

*Overnight: Galle*

### Day 8 – Departure

After breakfast, your chauffeur-guide drives you to the airport for your flight home.

*Drive: about 2 hours*

## 2. Classic Ceylon & Coast — 14 days / 13 nights

The complete first visit at an easy pace: three nights among the ancient cities, sacred Kandy, two nights in tea country, the mountain village of Ella and three days on a palm-fringed beach to finish.

**Best for:** first-time couples · **Best time:** December to April

**Zeilan signature experience:** village cycling and a home-cooked lunch with a farming family near Sigiriya.

**Optional on request:** swap the day 9 drive for the scenic hill-country train to Ella.

**Accommodation summary** (vehicle at disposal throughout; hotels to be chosen)

| Place | Nights | Hotel | Room type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Negombo | 1 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Sigiriya | 3 | To choose (e.g. Heritance Kandalama) | Double / twin |
| Kandy | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Nuwara Eliya | 2 | To choose (e.g. Heritance Tea Factory) | Double / twin |
| Ella | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Mirissa | 3 | To choose | Double / twin |

### Day 1 – Welcome to Sri Lanka

Your chauffeur-guide meets you at the airport and takes you to your hotel in Negombo, 20 minutes away. Rest after the flight and enjoy a sunset dinner by the sea.

*Drive: 20 minutes · Overnight: Negombo*

### Day 2 – Into the Cultural Triangle

Drive north through villages and paddy fields to Sigiriya, home to Sri Lanka's most famous ancient sites. Settle into your jungle hotel and spend the afternoon by the pool.

*Drive: about 4 hours · Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 3 – Sigiriya Rock and village life

Climb Sigiriya rock fortress at dawn, passing the frescoes and lion's paws on the way to the royal summit. Later, cycle through a nearby village, take a short ride on a traditional bullock cart or catamaran, and enjoy a home-cooked lunch with a local family.

*Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 4 – Polonnaruwa and elephant safari

Explore the medieval royal city of Polonnaruwa by bicycle, with its palaces, temples and the great carved Buddhas of Gal Vihara. In the afternoon, head out on a jeep safari in Minneriya or Kaudulla to watch wild elephants gather by the water.

*Drive: about 1.5 hours each way · Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 5 – Dambulla and Kandy

Visit the Dambulla cave temple with its ancient statues and painted ceilings, then stop at a spice garden on the way to Kandy. In the evening, attend the ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 6 – Kandy at leisure

Stroll through the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya, famous for their orchids and avenues of giant palms. Walk around Kandy Lake and the town in the afternoon, and watch a traditional Kandyan dance performance in the evening.

*Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 7 – Into tea country

Climb into the hills past waterfalls and tea estates to Nuwara Eliya. Stop at a working tea factory to see how Ceylon tea is made, with a tasting overlooking the plantations.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Nuwara Eliya*

### Day 8 – Horton Plains

Set off before dawn for Horton Plains National Park and walk the trail to World's End, where the land drops away nearly a kilometre to the plains below. Return to Nuwara Eliya for a relaxed afternoon by Gregory Lake.

*Overnight: Nuwara Eliya*

### Day 9 – Through the hills to Ella

A beautiful drive winds down through tea estates and mountain villages to Ella, with stops at viewpoints along the way. Spend the afternoon exploring the village's cafés and enjoying the valley views.

*Drive: about 2 hours · Overnight: Ella*

### Day 10 – Ella at leisure

Walk up Little Adam's Peak in the cool of the morning for sweeping views of the hills. Later, visit the Nine Arches Bridge and Ravana Falls, or simply relax.

*Overnight: Ella*

### Day 11 – Down to the coast

Leave the hills and drive south to Mirissa, a laid-back beach town lined with palms. Spend the afternoon on the sand.

*Drive: about 3.5 hours · Overnight: Mirissa*

### Day 12 – Whale watching

Join an early-morning boat trip with a responsible operator in search of blue whales and dolphins (optional). Spend the rest of the day on the beach or at Coconut Tree Hill.

*Overnight: Mirissa*

### Day 13 – Galle Fort

Take a short trip along the coast to Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk the ramparts, see the lighthouse and browse the boutiques, then return to Mirissa for a final sunset.

*Drive: 45 minutes each way · Overnight: Mirissa*

### Day 14 – Departure

After breakfast, your chauffeur-guide drives you to the airport for your flight home.

*Drive: about 2.5 hours*

## 3. Untamed Sri Lanka: Cloud Forests, Leopards & Whales — 12 days / 11 nights

Three national parks, the cloud forest of Horton Plains and the ocean: a journey built around Sri Lanka's wildlife, always watched responsibly with expert guides, and with two nights at almost every stop.

**Best for:** wildlife and nature lovers · **Best time:** February to April

**Zeilan signature experience:** a full-day private safari in Wilpattu with a naturalist tracker, far from the crowds.

**Accommodation summary** (vehicle at disposal throughout; hotels to be chosen)

| Place | Nights | Hotel | Room type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wilpattu | 2 | To choose (safari lodge) | Double / twin |
| Sigiriya | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Kandy | 1 | To choose | Double / twin |
| Nuwara Eliya | 2 | To choose (e.g. Heritance Tea Factory) | Double / twin |
| Yala | 2 | To choose (e.g. Cinnamon Wild Yala) | Double / twin |
| Mirissa | 2 | To choose | Double / twin |

### Day 1 – Arrive and head for Wilpattu

Your chauffeur-guide meets you at the airport and drives you north to a lodge on the edge of Wilpattu, Sri Lanka's largest national park. Unwind to the sounds of the forest.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Wilpattu*

### Day 2 – Wilpattu safari

Spend the day on safari in Wilpattu, a quiet park of natural lakes and dense forest, with a naturalist tracker. Search for leopards, sloth bears, spotted deer and a rich variety of birds, with far fewer jeeps than the better-known parks.

*Overnight: Wilpattu*

### Day 3 – To Sigiriya and the elephant gathering

Drive east to Sigiriya. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Minneriya or Kaudulla, where wild elephants come to graze and bathe by the reservoirs.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 4 – Sigiriya Rock and birdlife

Climb Sigiriya rock fortress at dawn, when the light is soft and the birds are active. Later, take a guided nature walk in the surrounding forest and villages.

*Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 5 – Kandy

Drive to Kandy, Sri Lanka's hill capital. Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic for the evening ceremony and walk around Kandy Lake.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 6 – Into the highlands

Climb into the hills past waterfalls and tea estates to Nuwara Eliya. In the afternoon, look for the hill country's endemic birds in Victoria Park.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Nuwara Eliya*

### Day 7 – Horton Plains cloud forest

Set off before dawn for Horton Plains National Park, a high plateau of grassland and cloud forest. Walk to World's End, where the cliff drops away nearly a kilometre, looking for sambar deer and endemic birds along the trail.

*Overnight: Nuwara Eliya*

### Day 8 – To Yala

Drive down from the hills through Ella and Wellawaya, stopping at Ravana Falls and the ancient Buduruwagala rock carvings, to the dry south-east, where the forest gives way to scrub and lagoons. Arrive at your lodge near Yala in time for sunset.

*Drive: about 4 hours · Overnight: Yala*

### Day 9 – Yala safari

Spend the day on safari in Yala National Park, home to one of the highest densities of leopards in the world. Look out for elephants, crocodiles, sloth bears and hundreds of bird species.

*Overnight: Yala*

### Day 10 – To the coast

After an optional early safari, drive west to the beach at Mirissa and spend the afternoon on the sand.

*Drive: about 2.5 hours · Overnight: Mirissa*

### Day 11 – Whale watching

Head out early with a responsible operator in search of blue whales, sperm whales and spinner dolphins. Spend the afternoon on the beach.

*Overnight: Mirissa*

### Day 12 – Departure

After breakfast, your chauffeur-guide drives you to the airport for your flight home.

*Drive: about 2.5 hours*

*Note: Kandy is the only one-night stop. Day 8 is about 4 hours on the road.*

Drive up the coast to Pasikuda, with its long, shallow bay of calm, clear water, ideal for young swimmers.

Drive inland to Sigiriya. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Kaudulla or Minneriya, where August is the peak of the great elephant gathering.

Climb Sigiriya rock fortress early in the morning. Later, cycle through a village, ride a bullock cart and help cook a traditional Sri Lankan lunch.

Drive north to Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka's first great capital. In the late afternoon, visit the vast white dagobas and the sacred Bo tree as pilgrims gather at sunset.

Drive south along the quiet north-east coast, stopping for lunch and a walk on the beach at Mullaitivu to break the journey. Arrive in Trincomalee, with its deep natural harbour and white-sand beaches.

*Drive: about 4.5 hours · Overnight: Trincomalee*

### Day 7 – Pigeon Island and Koneswaram

Snorkel among coral and reef fish at Pigeon Island National Park. Later, visit Koneswaram Temple on its cliff above the sea.

*Overnight: Trincomalee*

Drive inland to Sigiriya. In the afternoon, take a jeep safari in Kaudulla or Minneriya to watch wild elephants by the water.

*Drive: about 2.5 hours · Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 9 – Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa

Climb Sigiriya rock fortress at dawn, then explore the medieval city of Polonnaruwa by bicycle, including the great carved Buddhas of Gal Vihara.

*Overnight: Sigiriya*

### Day 10 – Dambulla and Kandy

Visit the Dambulla cave temple on the way to Kandy. In the evening, attend the ceremony at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

*Drive: about 3 hours · Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 11 – Kandy at leisure

Stroll through the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya, walk around Kandy Lake, and watch a Kandyan dance performance in the evening.

*Overnight: Kandy*

### Day 12 – A planter's bungalow

Drive into the tea hills to a colonial-era planter's bungalow near Hatton, with high tea on the lawn and views over the estates.

*Drive: about 2.5 hours · Overnight: Hatton*

### Day 13 – Walking the Pekoe Trail

Walk a guided stage of the Pekoe Trail through tea estates, villages and forest, with views over the Castlereagh reservoir.

*Overnight: Hatton*

### Day 14 – Adam's Peak or a slow day

In pilgrimage season (December to May), make the optional night climb of Adam's Peak for sunrise. Otherwise, enjoy a slow day on the estate with a tea factory visit.

*Overnight: Hatton*

### Day 15 – Departure

After breakfast, your chauffeur-guide drives you to the airport for your flight home.

*Drive: about 4 hours*

# Day tours

Eight private day tours, built around where clients stay: Colombo and Negombo, the south and west coast, and the Cultural Triangle. All include a private air-conditioned car, van or coach (depending on group size) with a guide, and hotel pickup.

## From Colombo or Negombo

**1. Colombo City & Culture** — half day, under 1 h drive

- Plan: Gangaramaya Temple, Pettah market by tuk-tuk, Old Dutch Hospital, sunset at Galle Face Green
- Best time: all year
- Photos: Pettah market colour, Gangaramaya, Galle Face sunset

**2. Kandy & the Temple of the Tooth** — full day, about 3 h each way

- Plan: spice garden en route, Temple of the Tooth, Kandy Lake, Peradeniya Botanical Gardens
- Best time: all year
- Photos: Temple of the Tooth, Kandy Lake, botanical gardens

**3. Sigiriya & Dambulla** — long full day, about 4 h each way

- Plan: early start, Sigiriya Rock, village lunch, Dambulla cave temple
- Best time: all year; mark as a long day
- Photos: Sigiriya from below and from the top, Dambulla cave paintings

## From the south and west coast

**4. Galle Fort Walking Tour** — half day, under 1 h from Galle, Unawatuna or Mirissa

- Plan: ramparts, lighthouse, Dutch church, boutiques and cafés, sunset on the walls
- Best time: Dec–Apr
- Photos: lighthouse, rampart walls, fort streets

**5. Udawalawe Elephant Safari** — full day, about 2.5 h each way from Mirissa or Galle

- Plan: Elephant Transit Home feeding time, then an afternoon jeep safari
- Best time: all year
- Photos: wild elephant herds, young elephants at the Transit Home (from the viewing area only)

**6. Whale Watching from Mirissa** — morning, short transfer

- Plan: early boat with a responsible operator, breakfast on board, beach afternoon
- Best time: Dec–Apr only
- Photos: blue whale tail, boat at sea at dawn

**7. Madu Ganga River & Turtle Conservation** — half day, under 1 h from Bentota

- Plan: boat through the mangroves, cinnamon island, a turtle conservation project
- Best time: all year (best Dec–Apr)
- Photos: mangrove channels, cinnamon peeling, turtles

## From Sigiriya, Habarana or Kandy

**8. Minneriya or Kaudulla Elephant Safari** — half day, under 1 h from Sigiriya

- Plan: afternoon jeep safari; the guide picks the park where the herds are
- Best time: all year; peak elephant gathering Jul–Oct
- Photos: large elephant herds at the reservoir
