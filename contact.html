Frequently Asked

Common Questions

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka can be visited year-round. The west and south coasts are best from November to March, while the east coast shines from April to September. We'll tailor your dates to the regions you'd like to explore.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance, especially for peak season travel (December–March). However, we can often accommodate last-minute requests — just get in touch.

Are your tours ATOL protected?

Yes. All flight-inclusive holidays booked through Zeilan Paradise are fully ATOL protected, giving you complete peace of mind and financial security.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Whether vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free or any other requirement — we arrange everything with hotels and restaurants in advance to ensure your comfort.

Do I need a visa to enter Sri Lanka?

UK citizens require an ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) to enter Sri Lanka — easily obtained online before travel. We'll guide you through the process as part of your booking.

What's included in your tours?

Our tours typically include private airport transfers, accommodation, certified local guides, private transport, selected meals, and key entrance fees. Full inclusions are detailed in each itinerary.
