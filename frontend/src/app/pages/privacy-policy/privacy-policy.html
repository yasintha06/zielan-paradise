Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Zeilan Paradise ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy notice will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website (regardless of where you visit it from) or book a bespoke journey with us, and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. The Data We Collect About You

Personal data, or personal information, means any information about an individual from which that person can be identified. We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you, including:

  • Identity Data: includes first name, last name, title, and passport details (if required for domestic flights/bookings).
  • Contact Data: includes billing address, email address and telephone numbers.
  • Financial Data: includes payment card details (processed securely via our payment gateways).
  • Transaction Data: includes details about payments to and from you and other details of products and services you have purchased from us.
  • Travel Profile Data: includes your interests, dietary requirements, health/mobility conditions (explicitly provided by you to ensure safe travel), and feedback.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

  • Where we need to perform the contract we are about to enter into or have entered into with you (e.g., booking your accommodation and private chauffeur).
  • Where it is necessary for our legitimate interests (or those of a third party) and your interests and fundamental rights do not override those interests.
  • Where we need to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

4. Enquiries Made Through This Website

When you use our contact form or journey planner, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, party size, interests and any message. We use them only to reply to you and to plan your journey.

  • Where it is stored: in our secure database (MongoDB Atlas) and our business email inbox. The website itself is hosted on Microsoft Azure.
  • Who can see it: only the Zeilan Paradise team. We never sell your details or use them for advertising.
  • How long we keep it: only for as long as we need it. Enquiries that don’t lead to a booking are deleted after 24 months.

5. Cookies & Browser Storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and our fonts and images are served from our own servers. We only store small, essential settings in your browser (for example, so the opening animation is shown once per visit). You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

6. Disclosures of Your Personal Data

We may have to share your personal data with the parties set out below for the purposes of fulfilling your tailor-made journey:

  • Ground Handlers & Chauffeurs: Our certified local team in Sri Lanka to ensure smooth operations.
  • Accommodation Providers & Airlines: To secure your bookings and flights.
  • Professional Advisers: Including lawyers, bankers, auditors and insurers based in the UK and Sri Lanka.

7. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered or disclosed. In addition, we limit access to your personal data to those employees, agents, contractors and other third parties who have a business need to know.

8. Your Legal Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of your personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data.
  • Object to processing of your personal data.

If you wish to exercise any of the rights set out above, please contact our UK-based concierge team via the Contact Us page.
