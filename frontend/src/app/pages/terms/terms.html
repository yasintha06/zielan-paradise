4. Cancellation Policy

If you or any member of your party needs to cancel your holiday once it has been confirmed, the lead name must immediately advise us in writing. Cancellation charges will be applied based on the date the written cancellation is received:

  • 61 days or more before departure: Loss of deposit.
  • 31 - 60 days before departure: 50% of total itinerary cost.
  • 15 - 30 days before departure: 75% of total itinerary cost.
  • 14 days or less before departure: 100% of total itinerary cost.

It is a strict condition of booking with Zeilan Paradise that you and all members of your party are covered by comprehensive travel insurance. Your insurance must cover medical expenses, repatriation, and cancellation charges. We will request proof of your policy details before your departure.
