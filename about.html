Who We Are

A Different Kind of Travel Company

Zeilan Paradise was founded on a simple belief: that travel to Sri Lanka should be as extraordinary as the island itself. Born from a passion for authentic, deeply personal experiences, we are a premium Destination Management Company connecting UK travellers with the true soul of Sri Lanka.

We are not a booking engine or a package holiday provider. We are your concierge, your advisor, your trusted local expert — based in the UK for your convenience, with an expert ground team living and breathing Sri Lanka every single day. Every itinerary we craft is unique, every hotel is hand-selected, and every experience is curated with purpose.

From the misty highlands of Ella to the ancient spires of Anuradhapura, from the golden shores of Mirissa to the wildlife of Yala — we know Sri Lanka intimately, and we can't wait to share it with you.
